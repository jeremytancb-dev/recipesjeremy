About Us

Two cuisines, one table

Recipe's Secret started as a shared notebook between two friends — one who grew up in a kitchen full of soy sauce and star anise, the other raised on olive oil and oregano. We found that the two cuisines share the same soul: fresh ingredients, bold aromatics, and recipes meant to be cooked for people you love.

Every recipe on this site is tested in a home kitchen, written in plain language, and built around ingredients you can actually find. No fuss, no filler — just the secret to really good food.
